# Step Challenge App

## The idea

This mobile app prototype makes step tracking more social. A person can log steps manually, follow progress, and take part in step challenges with other people. On a supported iPhone, it also includes an optional Apple Health connection for importing step totals with the user's permission.

## Product choices

- Keep manual logging available so the core challenge experience is not limited to one phone or health-data integration.
- Put individual progress and shared challenge progress in the same app.
- Request Health access only for the optional iPhone sync path.

The repository represents a prototype, not a released fitness or medical product. Its source and service configuration remain private.
