# Futureproof

## The problem

Experienced professionals often find AI learning either too generic or too technical to apply to their work. Futureproof is a learning platform designed to connect role-specific learning with practical use.

## What it does

Learners can explore role-focused learning paths, ask for a focused brief, study longer courses, and build artifacts that demonstrate what they have learned. The workspace includes progress tracking and study tools such as notes, quizzes, and review materials.

## Product choices

- Treat generated learning content as something that can become outdated. Keep its sources and history visible, revisit material that may have changed, and use learner feedback to find confusing or inaccurate sections.
- Connect readiness work to concrete evidence, such as completed learning missions and portfolio artifacts.
- Keep the product focused on learning and demonstrated work. It does not predict hiring outcomes or guarantee that a learner will get a job.

Futureproof is an evolving product. Its source code and operational configuration remain private.
